01 About

Bridging the shop floor and enterprise software.

I am a Manufacturing Execution Systems (MES) professional with 6+ years of experience delivering MES software development and implementation aligned with ISA-95 standards across discrete and batch manufacturing industries. I have worked with global customers across Europe and APAC, helping manufacturers bridge operational technology and IT to achieve better visibility, traceability, and production efficiency.

My expertise covers end-to-end MES initiatives — from requirement gathering, factory modeling (physical, process, and execution models), solution design, to implementation and integration. I have delivered solutions in material consumption and validation, workflow and task enforcement, production dispatching, traceability, maintenance management, quality operations, and enterprise integrations with ERP, APS, and other manufacturing platforms.

With a strong background in Mechatronics Engineering combined with solid software engineering skills, I focus on translating complex manufacturing challenges into scalable, practical digital solutions. I thrive in multicultural project environments where collaboration, adaptability, and clear communication are essential.

I am passionate about innovation that solves real operational problems, helping people achieve their goals, continuously learning new technologies, and simplifying complexity into effective, sustainable systems.
